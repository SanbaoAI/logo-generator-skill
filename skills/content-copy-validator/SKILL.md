# Content Copy Validator

Use this skill before visual design and again before final delivery. It verifies that the words are accurate, clear, consistent, and safe enough to design around.

## Workflow Role

Run after Product Manager intake and before Design Director strategy:

```text
PM Brief
-> Content Copy Validator
-> Design Director
-> Brand / Color / Typography / UI
-> Final Copy QA
```

## Responsibilities

Validate:

- Acronyms and terminology.
- Company, product, role, and feature names.
- Business claims and value propositions.
- Chinese clarity and professional tone.
- Mixed Chinese-English consistency.
- CTA wording.
- Repeated section titles and labels.
- Claims that may need evidence, citations, or softer wording.

## Required Checks

Return:

- `Terminology Review`
- `Fact And Claim Review`
- `Naming Consistency`
- `Chinese Copy Review`
- `Mixed-Language Review`
- `CTA Review`
- `Risky Or Unsupported Claims`
- `Approved Copy Rules`
- `Corrections Required`

## Enterprise AI Copy Rules

- Expand acronyms on first use.
- Keep FDE as `Forward Deployed Engineer` for role/person, and `Forward Deployed Engineering` for the practice/system.
- Avoid vague claims like "redefine the future" unless backed by concrete proof.
- Distinguish agent, workflow, model, tool, and platform.
- Use "部署", "运营", "评估", "权限", "日志", "可观测" carefully and consistently.
- Do not overpromise autonomy when human approval or governance is required.
- Use Chinese for primary business value when the audience is Chinese; use English for product/technical labels only when helpful.
