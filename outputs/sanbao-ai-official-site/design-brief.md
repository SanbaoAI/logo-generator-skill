# SanBaoTech Official Website Redesign Brief

## Product Manager Brief

- Company: 三宝国 / SanBaoTech
- Category: enterprise AI implementation studio
- Business: enterprise AI efficiency, Forward Deployed Engineering, AIGC content production, Hermes Agent engineering
- Target users: founders, CEOs, operations leaders, digital transformation teams, marketing/content teams, internal tool owners
- Core value: turn AI from scattered experiments into deployed, monitored, repeatable business workflows
- First-screen message: SanBaoTech builds AI operating systems for real enterprise workflows

## Content Copy Validation

- FDE approved expansion: Forward Deployed Engineer for role/person; Forward Deployed Engineering for practice/system.
- Avoid: Field Deployment Engineering.
- Claims must stay tied to deployment, observability, governance, evaluation, and operating metrics.
- Chinese value propositions should remain primary; English technical labels support the system surface.

## Design Director Strategy

The website should feel like a frontier AI deployment company, not a traditional consulting studio. Borrow the principles, not the look, of top technology sites:

- OpenAI: first viewport includes a usable-looking product/action surface.
- Anthropic: capability is paired with trust, control, and calm mission language.
- Tesla: large contrast, engineering scale, decisive CTAs, and visible deployment seriousness.

Design thesis: **A premium AI deployment cockpit for companies that want AI to enter real operations.**

## Brand Design

- Keep the SanBaoTech black symbol and red underline as brand memory.
- Use the three rising logo blades as a metaphor for Learn / Deploy / Compound.
- Avoid overly decorative AI imagery. Show system surfaces, process topology, command bars, and deployment status.

## Color Design

- Base: near-black graphite and cold white.
- Signal colors: SanBao red for decisive action, electric cyan for AI/system activity.
- Support: muted steel, cool gray, faint grid lines.
- Avoid beige-heavy paper feeling and purple-blue generic AI gradients.

## Typography Design

- Display type: strong system sans-serif, not editorial serif, to avoid an old consulting-deck feeling.
- Chinese/English hierarchy: brand name can be split across two lines; value statement gets its own line instead of being forced into the same headline.
- Type rhythm: large hero, compact system labels, readable Chinese body copy, and short console labels.
- Line-height: tighter for display, relaxed for explanatory body copy.
- Mobile: planned line breaks, no long mixed Chinese-English single-line collisions.
- QA: hero text must not collide with the AI console; labels must remain legible in dark panels.

## Japanese Typography Consultant

- Use Japanese-inspired ma spacing: leave stronger air around key Chinese sentences.
- Treat Chinese headlines as composed blocks. Prefer semantic line breaks over automatic wrapping.
- Keep English labels small and supportive; Chinese value propositions stay dominant.
- Use grid discipline rather than decorative effects to create premium feeling.
- Avoid copying Japanese brand style; borrow restraint, rhythm, and placement logic.

## Visual Asset Direction

- Hero: AI deployment cockpit with command input and active workflow panels.
- Business lines: four compact capability lanes.
- System section: Forward Deployed Engineering pipeline from discovery to deployment to monitoring.
- Trust layer: governance, evaluation, human approval, logs.

## UI Design

- First screen: split between large identity and operating cockpit.
- Second screen: immediate business capability proof.
- Middle: full-width dark engineering band for scale.
- Later: trust/control layer and brand system support.
- CTA: start from one enterprise workflow, not vague consultation.

## Design Director Review

Approved direction: the redesign must look more advanced by replacing repeated pale cards with a visible AI system, sharper contrast, more confident spacing, and more specific enterprise AI artifacts.
